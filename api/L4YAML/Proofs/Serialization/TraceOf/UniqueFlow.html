    Trace uniqueness over the FLOW fragment (EVENTSOF-CONCEPT §10, slice 2) #

    Capstone chain: Blueprint 7.7 parse_iff_grammar ← TraceWellFormed has one trace per input ← traceOf_unique ← this flow fragment.

    Two derivations of one span may use different constructors with the same trace (["a": b] is pairValue or pairJsonAdjacentValue) and may split the span at different intermediate positions, so the induction carries span determinism under the flow follow-set: for each member T of the flow fragment, T n c s m₁ ev₁ → T n c s m₂ ev₂ → follow m₁ → follow m₂ → m₁ = m₂ ∧ ev₁ = ev₂ (NodeDet, ContentDet, JsonDet, CrossDet, SeqDet, MapDet, SeqEntriesDet, MapEntriesDet), proved together by strong induction on the length of the remaining input (allDet). The same-end lemmas tFlowNode_unique, tFlowContent_unique, tCFlowJsonNode_unique, tCFlowJsonContent_unique, tFlowSequence_unique, tFlowMapping_unique, tCNsProperties_unique, tCNsTagProperty_unique, tCNsAnchorProperty_unique, tCNsAliasNode_unique are the corollaries with the trivial follow. Entries and entry lists are not prefix-free (inside [a:b] the span a: is the plain node a: or the key a with an empty value), so tFlowSeqEntry_unique, tFlowMapEntry_unique, tFlowSeqEntries_unique, tFlowMapEntries_unique carry the follow (EFollow, resp. CloseStop) that the enclosing collection always provides.

    The leaf span-determinism lemmas are where the proof lives: separators (sep_det), anchors/aliases/tags (anchor_det, alias_det, tag_det), quoted scalars (dq_det, sq_det), plain scalars including multi-line flow continuation (plain_det, under FStop), properties (props_det, under PStop).
