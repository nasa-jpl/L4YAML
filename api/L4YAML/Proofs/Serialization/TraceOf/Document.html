    Event-annotated documents and stream (EVENTSOF-CONCEPT §10) #

    Hand-written twins of Surface/Document.lean's productions. Two orderings are fixed here rather than read off the text: a document's beginDocument precedes the declareTags of the %TAG directives that textually precede its --- (RuntimeScopes.documentEvents), and ... is endDocument. SLYamlStream.scannerDrop has no twin: the annotated stream is the intended grammar, so scan ok → TLYamlStream … (S3) is available only once Row 12 retires the drop.
