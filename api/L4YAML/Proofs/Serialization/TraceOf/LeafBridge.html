    S3 slice (1) — the leaf bridge (EVENTSOF §10, capstone 7.7 parse_iff_grammar) #

    The scanner's .anchor name / .alias name / .tag handle suffix tokens against the annotated leaf relations of TraceOf/Leaves.lean: the lexeme the scanner consumed is a derivation of the leaf relation whose trace is exactly TokenEvents.tokenEvent of the emitted token. The surface derivation is the *_prod lemmas' (StructureProduction/NodeProduction); what is new is the payload: the anchor name IS the consumed span (collectAnchorNameLoop_chars), and a tag's handle IS handleOf of its span, arm by arm. Rule 2 first: Priority 100 (Tests/AdversarialInstantiation.lean).
