    The depth-0 pending node, T side (item 527, β PR 1) #

    PendingNode (StreamAccum) is what the accumulation invariant holds open at depth 0 beside the closed stream; its stream-level constructors — noPending, pendingDocEnd, pendingDirective, pendingDocStart — carry ROUTES into SLYamlStream. TPendingNode is their twin on the trace side: what the park holds open of the trace (ev_o, the open document's events) and of goOut's state (p, the pending %TAG handles), and the route that closes them into TLYamlStream — TSLYamlStream (DocBridge §α) carries the closed stream and the coupling. Priority 136: every depth-0 corpus prefix, by park class, against the goOut split.

    The content parks (pendingContent, pendingProps, pendingBlock, pendingMapValue, pendingBlockContent) are the next PR's; pendingFlow stays STARVED until Row 12 retires SLYamlStream.scannerDrop.
