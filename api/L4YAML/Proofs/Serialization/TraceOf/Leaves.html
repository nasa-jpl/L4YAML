    Event-annotated grammar — combinators and leaves (EVENTSOF-CONCEPT §10) #

    TraceOf/Node.lean (generated) is the copy of Surface/Node.lean's mutual block with one more index, the trace. This file supplies what the copy refers to: the annotated combinators (TSeq, TOpt, …, mirroring GSeq, GOpt, …), Lift for the productions that carry no event, and the leaves where events arise — tag and anchor properties, and aliases. A leaf's events are a function of the span of that one lexeme (tagEvents, anchorEvents, aliasEvents), never of a wider text: the s-separate between two properties may hold a comment, and a verbatim URI may hold anything, so the whole c-ns-properties span is not scanned (review of the first cut). Leaf traces are unique by construction; uniqueness of the whole is the grammar's business.
