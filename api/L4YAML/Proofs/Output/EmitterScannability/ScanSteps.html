    ∃ (h_size : m < s'.tokens.size), s'.tokens[m] = s.tokens[m]

    Chain wrapper: a NoColonDispatchChain preserves the token at every position m < s.tokens.size. Direct induction on the predicate — each step's non-: witness feeds the §G.2 capstone scanNextToken_at_non_colon_preserves_positions, which gives unconditional per-step preservation, transitively composed.

    Parallel to FlowMonoChain_preserves_position_specific_flow (substrate.e §E.6) and SavedKeyDoesntResolve_preserves_position_target (substrate.f §F.3), but free of both the FlowNoOverwriteAt side-condition and the single-target restriction: it covers the whole live prefix at once. This is the wrapper the blueprint sketched as FlowMonoChain_preserves_position_when_no_colon_dispatch.
