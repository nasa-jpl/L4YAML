    Acceptance — plain scalars, one line (Stream E) #

    Scanner converses of scanPlainScalar_prod: a narrowed [133] ns-plain-one-line derivation at the scanner's position makes scanPlainScalar succeed and land at the end of the derivation's trailing white run, emitting the trimmed .plain token.

    The unnarrowed converse is false (probes in Tests/Guards/Proofs/AcceptanceProbes.lean):

    • a #b — the scanner reads # as a comment and stops at a; [132]'s hash entry agrees (# is content only with an EMPTY s-white*), and PlainCharOk.hash is likewise admitted only with no pending white.
    • --- x / --- — [126]'s dashSafe derives --- at column 0; the scanner sees a document marker and emits an empty scalar without moving (→ hdoc).
    • a — [133] ends at a; the scanner buffers the trailing whites and lands after them (→ the landing is sp'', the end of the GStar SSWhite run).
