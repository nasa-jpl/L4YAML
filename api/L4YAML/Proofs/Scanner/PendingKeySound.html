    The explicit-key register IS the innermost pending key (DOCS item 338) #

    Item 335 gave every indentation level one pendingKeys slot and made the scanner re-read the register (explicitKeyLine, explicitKeyCol) off the innermost marked level whenever a level closes. Item 337's hook closes a level on a dedenting -. What those two items bought is a TOKEN-LEVEL invariant, stated and carried here:

    • SlotsOk — pendingKeys is in lockstep with indents, and a marked slot sits on a level of column ≥ 0 (never the sentinel);
    • RegOk — outside flow, the register pair equals PendingKeys.innermost;
    • BaseOk — inside flow, the pair saved at the OUTERMOST flow open (the bottom of explicitKeyStack) equals the innermost pending key, which no flow token moves (the block writers are !inFlow-guarded) — lane (d) of item 334;
    • StackOk — explicitKeyStack has one saved pair per open flow level.

    It is a property of the state BETWEEN tokens, not of every intermediate state: unwindIndents pops marked levels before the stale-key hook re-reads the register (which is why it is not a ScannerSurfCorr field — item 338 priced and refuted that shape), and the one hook exemption that survives a pop is the ? the next scanKey overwrites. So preprocess_sound concludes RegOk only for c ≠ '?', and scanKey_sound needs no RegOk premise.
