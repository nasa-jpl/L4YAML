    Flow-stack synchrony over the scanner loop (capstone 7.7, stream S3, obligation β2a) #

    The three scanner facts the Shape loop invariant of DocStartLive needs and that the tree had only at the primitive level:

    • FlowSync s := s.flowLevel = s.flowStack.size ∧ s.simpleKeyStack.size = s.flowStack.size — the two synchrony clauses of ScannerState.WellFormed — holds at mk' and is preserved by every scanNextToken step (FlowSync_scanNextToken) and so by scanLoopFull (FlowSync_scanLoopFull);
    • a step taken in flow context leaves indents unchanged (scanNextToken_indents_of_inFlow);
    • skipToContent never clears simpleKeyAllowed (skipToContent_simpleKeyAllowed_mono): its only write is := true at a line break in block context.

    Adversarial grid: Priority 93.
