    A content park awaits no node (item 352, Rule 2) #

    What the scanner does with the line AFTER a complete content node. The node-domained value-line twin accum_block_on_closeThenBlock.h_valFV asks a content park (pendingContent, pendingBlockContent) what the stack is once a block node is scanned at the park — and no content park can answer it: a new-line landing at a column greater than the node's start is invalidBareDocument (the bare-document check, h_bare at every step), a column-0 - is a sibling entry (the - arm's nn < k is refuted at k = 0), and a plain scalar FOLDS its continuation lines into itself, so no second node ever starts at the park. Pinned here so the ∨ True alternative item 352 gave h_valFV stays a measured fact, not a recollection.
