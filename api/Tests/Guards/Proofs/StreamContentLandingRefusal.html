A landed key at the sentinel takes that arm, and "x"⏎b: 2 is refused before its : exists.

    This item guards those four and measures the one raw site it deliberately leaves standing: content_dispatch_after_close's bareNodeRoute, whose refutable half is EMPTY.

    §1 is the half the refusal closes, read at the KEY side. §2 is the half it does not. §3 is the empty site, and the two DIFFERENT reasons its three callers give for being empty. §4 is the guarded routes and the narrowing at their types. §5 is what remains.
