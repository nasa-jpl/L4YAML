                POSITIVE — the deep guard descends as a pure restriction #

                deep_descend is the toy of flowBodyContentDeep_descend: transport Deep from [lo, hi) into the nested interior [k+1, j) whose opener is at k. The child head hd (k+1) is the parent's opener field at k; the child's opener/separator fields are the parent's quantifiers restricted.
