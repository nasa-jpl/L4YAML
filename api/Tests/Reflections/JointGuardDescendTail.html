        The descend-tail at the folded guard. Parametric in the close map, the guard predicates, the two fixed outer carriers CarrierS/CarrierM, the deferred map fact M2, the marker balance Bal, the separator Sep, and ONE abstract edge:

        • carrierEdge — the carrier-fed concrete descend (R532 analog): given the window, the two close-gated deep/enclosure HALVES, the close disjunction, the two carriers, M2, the frame bounds and the marker facts, it advances those halves to the suffix [m+1, hi).
