    Reflection 434 — a refutation-driven guard-strengthening threads contravariantly: producer-first #

    Self-contained (core Lean, no L4YAML import) toy of the R434 step.

    Context. R433 refuted a per-window universal ∀ window, (guards) → Deliverable window by exhibiting a guard-satisfying window the deliverable can't inhabit; the fix is to ADD a guard H (the Dyck floor) that excludes it. That universal flows across a PRODUCER → CONSUMER boundary: a producer CONCLUDES it (from its own windowFacts-style hypothesis), a consumer HYPOTHESIZES it (and instantiates it at located windows).

    The finding. The added guard must be applied at BOTH ends, and the two ends have OPPOSITE cost, by the contravariance of →:

    • PRODUCER (concludes ∀ x, G x → Deliv x): adding H to the conclusion is FREE. The producer RECEIVES the new premise H x on its conclusion side and passes it straight to its own hypothesis (which also gains H). Pure threading — no new proof obligation.
    • CONSUMER (hypothesizes ∀ x, G x → Deliv x): adding H makes the hypothesis WEAKER, so at every instantiation the consumer must now SUPPLY H x. That is the real work.

    So do the PRODUCER side first (free — it pins the guard's exact shape), then pay for the CONSUMER side.

    The toy below: a producer that threads the new guard H for free, and a consumer that must manufacture H 3 to instantiate the strengthened hypothesis.
